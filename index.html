How the game works

Do contribution & verification work → earn energy. Your energy score is your leaderboard rank. Each round, the top 10 by energy get a head start for the next round only (it never stacks); #11 and below start at zero. No head start in the very first round.

  1. +20#1
  2. +18#2
  3. +16#3
  4. +14#4
  5. +12#5
  6. +10#6
  7. +8#7
  8. +6#8
  9. +4#9
  10. +2#10
  11. 0#11+

#1 = +20 and top 10 > 0 are the owner's rule; the steps in between (−2 each) are a provisional assumption. Season score: each round, the top 10 by energy also earn provisional season points (25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1); the Championship ranks the season total. Spending energy on championships and decay of hoarded energy are planned but not yet applied.

Sample data: placeholder accounts, not real members. Energy is earned by contribution/verification work, never follows/RTs; seed bonus only amplifies effort (0 effort ⇒ 0). Rules are provisional. No $ or token values.
