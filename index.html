How the game works

Do contribution & verification work → earn Credits (100 scoring points = 1 Credit — display only, not an airdrop rate). More Credits = higher rank.

Two rewards: (1) Season 1 BNB prizes for the top 3 — illustrative 1 BNB pool, ≈$300 / $200 / $100 split (allocation weights 50% / 33.33% / 16.67%; not funded or promised). (2) Credits earned during the campaign convert into airdrops. The conversion rate will be announced after Season 1 closes, once season performance is reviewed.

Each round, the top 10 get a head start for the next round only (never stacks, none in the first round, needs your own work that round):

  1. +25#1–5
  2. +15#6–10
  3. 0#11+

Head start is in points: +25 = +0.25 Credit, +15 = +0.15 Credit. Season points (Championship tab): each round’s top 10 earn provisional season points (25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1). Spending Credits on championships and decay of hoarded Credits are planned, not yet applied.
