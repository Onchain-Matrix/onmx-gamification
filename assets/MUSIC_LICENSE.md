# Hero background music

- Title: original synthwave bed (untitled)
- Tempo: 128 BPM
- Key: F# minor
- Length: 60.000 seconds (32 bars of 4/4), designed as a seamless loop
- Arrangement: four-on-the-floor kick, snare on 2 and 4, 16th hi-hats, sidechained sub and saw bass arpeggio, pulsing detuned pad, sparse lead in the second half, occasional low taiko-like hits every 8 bars

This track is an original composition, generated programmatically with Python (NumPy oscillators and envelopes, then ffmpeg loudnorm). No third-party recordings, stems, or copyrighted songs were used.
